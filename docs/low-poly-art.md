# Rooftop art direction

The interactive scene uses locally authored Three.js geometry: apartment buildings, rooftop platform, hut, water tank, planters, trees, bunting, Auntie Mei, adjustable soil, fence segments and harvested plants. These assets respond to the reader's dimensions. No external 3D model downloads are required.

The complementary image in `public/img/rooftop-low-poly.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the imagegen skill. It is used for the WebGL fallback; the opening image was removed at Keith's request. The selected PNG was resized to 1280 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at quality 86 using Sharp.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished low-poly environment artwork for the opening of an educational garden game on a personal portfolio. Primary request: a beautiful isometric miniature Hong Kong rooftop community garden, like a carefully art-directed cozy 3D indie game diorama. Scene: a warm cream rooftop platform on a pastel coral mid-rise building, with small pale teal, dusty peach and buttery-yellow Hong Kong apartment towers receding behind it. Main subject: one fenced rectangular vegetable garden with dark soil, small faceted cabbage plants, terracotta pots, a rooftop shed and a cheerful small low-poly gardener wearing a broad straw hat and teal dress. Matte faceted geometry with clean edges, subtle bevels, sophisticated soft ambient occlusion, warm morning sunlight and long gentle shadows. Composition: landscape 3:2 artwork, the rooftop garden as the single clear focal point, plenty of airy pale mint and cream space around the floating diorama; everything fits in frame. Palette: cream #f6f1e7, terracotta coral #e4572e, deep teal #1f7a6d, sage, warm straw gold. Game-ready visual language with very readable simple silhouettes; detailed enough to feel handcrafted but no photorealism, no voxel cubes, no busy city clutter. No words, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Render as a finished illustration, not a screenshot of UI.
