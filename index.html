07 · Experience

Product, engineering and the classroom.

Product Architect at Gamenoodlesoup, split between engineering and product: client work, the specification, and most of the build. AI talks for teachers and principals. Certified speaker, Hong Kong Productivity Council.

  • Defined the product, with occasional advice from the manager. Set the direction and what to build next. Shows a teacher and a student how one maths skill depends on another, and where that student is.
  • Structured the Hong Kong curriculum into concepts, the skill each concept needs first, and the mistakes students actually make, so a wrong answer traces back to the real gap.
  • Sat in on lessons at more than 50 schools, then discussed with the maths teacher and the principal where it worked in the classroom and where it did not.
  • Turned a principal's request, to see students' working, into a live dashboard: the classwork each student is on, and the error behind a wrong answer. Speed and accuracy had to hold at the same time.
  • Started the practice game as collecting items, so maths practice felt like a game rather than homework. After teachers tried it, grew it into daily-life challenges, so the maths does not stay on the worksheet. Daily practice rose after that.
  • Wrote the requirement documents for the programmers, and built the part that reads handwritten working.
  • Founded in July 2023. Still running, and separate from Gamenoodlesoup. Classes booked directly by parents.
  • Held more than 40 classes. Students set up their own experiments from daily life, instead of learning science from a textbook.
  • Built the member app for Otsuka's Pocari Sweat brand over about six months. Just launched, so member numbers are not in yet.
  • Worked through the client's needs in meetings, built a prototype, and reviewed it with them. Wrote the full requirements document.
  • Designed and built the system: the app, the data storage, the APIs and the content system. Covers events, ads, campaigns, rewards, a daily check-in and stock.
  • Added stock-taking and a redemption limit on top of the requested points reward, so staff can run it themselves.
  • Built receipt recognition that reads the product code on a receipt and checks it against the database, to confirm the items bought are real.
  • Designed the system and managed the project for the interview day.
  • Covers the whole interview day: QR-code check-in, an AI interview that records the voice and checks pronunciation, and a message to the parent when it is time to collect the child.
  • Ran 8,000 students through in 8 hours, with one record lost.
  • Wrote the proposal, pitched it, and won funding from HKSIE, the Hong Kong government's Social Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Fund.
  • Pairs a student with an older adult to design and build a small gadget for daily use, using programming and 3D printing.
  • Ran the whole programme for more than 30 students and more than 30 older adults. Now applying for the next round of funding.
  • Teaches older adults to use a phone by breaking each action into a gesture, then into a game.
  • Designed the tracking separately from the games: finger position, press force and reaction time. Built the platform that collects and analyses that data. The team built the app.
  • About 2,800 older adults took the course. The app has been downloaded more than 20,000 times.
